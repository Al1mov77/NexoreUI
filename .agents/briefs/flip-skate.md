# Identity Brief: FLIP Skate Shop

## 1. Meta
- **Template ID**: `flip-skate` / `template-flip-skate-pro`
- **Archetype**: D Product Sheet / Skate Workshop
- **Category**: E-Commerce / Hardware Studio

## 2. Design Language & Tokens
- **Display Font**: `Unbounded` (700, 800, 900, tight tracking -0.04em)
- **Body Font**: `Manrope` (400, 500, 600, 700)
- **Palette**:
  - Hero Green: `#869833` (exact color of video backdrop)
  - Lime Accent: `#c4d454`
  - Ink: `#14180a` (deep slate olive black)
  - Paper: `#f3f5e9` (light creamy warm bone)
  - Accent Red/Orange: `#d9420b` (for primary buttons, step numbers)
  - Dark Mode: `#11140a` bg / `#eef2dc` ink (hero & CTA stay `#869833`)
- **Surfaces**: Solid tactile paper/card surfaces with 2px ink borders and 6px radius (`rounded-[6px] border-2 border-[#14180a]`).
- **Motion**:
  - 240-frame scroll-scrubbed canvas with 0.1 lerp smoothing
  - 4 crossfading chapters synced to scroll quarters
  - Hardware part jump buttons with lock window
  - CSS ticker marquee
  - Scroll-snap horizontal product carousel with interactive +/- quantity stepper

## 3. Story
- **What it is**: Direct-from-workshop skate shop building custom boards and shipping hardware parts.
- **What the user feels**: The physical weight, pop, and mechanical precision of a skateboard coming together.
- **Signature moment**: The board flips into the air, separates into exploded deck, trucks, and wheels, and lands back down rolling smoothly.
