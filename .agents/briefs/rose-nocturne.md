# Identity brief: rose-nocturne

Save as `.agents/briefs/rose-nocturne.md`.

## Story (3 lines)
1. Product and visitor: Rose Nocturne is an extraction atelier pioneering sub-zero cryogenic floral maceration of Damascena roses; the visitor is an olfactive connoisseur, cosmetic chemist, or discerning luxury patron evaluating the extraction methodology.
2. What the visitor must feel or decide: Feel the clinical precision and quiet monastic luxury of nocturnal harvest extraction, inspect the 4-phase cryogenic sequence, and reserve from the seasonal allocation.
3. Signature moment shows: S5 Pinned horizontal sequence tracking the 4 physical extraction stages (Midnight Harvest, Cryo-Suspension, Isochoric Extraction, Obsidian Maturation) seamlessly across viewport scroll.

## Identity (10 axes)
1. Archetype (A-L): K ceremonial centered (monumental editorial stature, centered Roman serif headline, pure obsidian noir backdrop, tactile phase progression tabs).
2. Grid and density: Container max-w-7xl, 16px to 24px gutter, 8pt base unit, section vertical padding 112px desktop / 80px mobile.
3. Display type: Cinzel (`family=Cinzel:wght@500;600;700;800&family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@300;400;500;600&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap`).
4. Body and aux type: Plus Jakarta Sans for body text; JetBrains Mono for metadata, phase indicators, and metrics.
5. Palette (hex): P5 Plum Noir
   - bg: #000000 / surface: #09090b / card: #0d0d11 / border: rgba(255,255,255,0.12) / text: #ffffff / muted: #a1a1aa / accent: #f43f5e (Rose).
6. Surface language: SL2 Soft cards (radius 16px-24px, 1px subtle border, deep dark elevation).
7. Motion personality: M7 Snappy mechanical / high-precision pinned scroll.
8. Signature moment: S5 Pinned horizontal extraction sequence with 1:1 hardware scroll tracking and tactile stage tab navigation.
9. Copy voice: Monastic, clinical, editorial. Zero AI buzzwords, zero fake urgency, zero telemetry jargon.
10. Imagery treatment: Deep atmospheric photography and authentic cinematic video loop (`/videos/rose-nocturne-atelier.mp4`) with smooth viewport intersection observation.
